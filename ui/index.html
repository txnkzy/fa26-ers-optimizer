    How it works

    Four steps, and the one everybody gets wrong is the last.

    1. Drive

      A few clean laps in one session, at a pace you can repeat. The logger writes each lap as you cross the line; invalidated laps and in/out laps are thrown away.

    2. Run the optimizer

      It fits how your car accelerates, brakes and grips from those laps, then searches for where to spend the battery. A couple of minutes, and you can go back to the game while it works.

    3. Save into a setup

      Pick one of your setups for this circuit. The old file is copied to .bak first, and only deployment maps 1 to 3 are touched.

    4. Restart the session

      Assetto Corsa only reads a setup when the car loads. A map saved while you are sitting on track never reaches the car, and reloading the setup in the pit menu is not enough.

    The three maps

    STRAT 1

    Qualifying

    Spends the whole battery over one lap. Arrive with it full.

    STRAT 2

    Race

    Ends the lap on the charge it started, so it can repeat.

    STRAT 3

    Recharge

    Puts charge back in. Meant to feel slow.

    Switch between them with STRAT Map on the pit setup screen. The PU mode switch on the wheel does not change it.
