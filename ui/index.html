      How it works

      Four steps, and the one everybody gets wrong is the last.

      1. Drive

        A few clean laps in one session, at a pace you can repeat. The logger writes each lap as you cross the line; invalidated laps and in/out laps are thrown away.

      2. Run the optimizer

        It fits how your car accelerates, brakes and grips from those laps, then searches for where to spend the battery. Usually well under a minute, and you can go back to the game while it works.

      3. Save into a setup

        Pick one of your setups for this circuit. The old file is copied to .bak first, and only the deployment maps are touched.

      4. Restart the session

        Assetto Corsa only reads a setup when the car loads. A map saved while you are sitting on track never reaches the car, and reloading the setup in the pit menu is not enough.

      The three main maps

      STRAT 1

      Qualifying

      Spends the whole battery over one lap. Arrive with it full.

      STRAT 2

      Race

      Ends the lap on the charge it started, so it can repeat.

      STRAT 3

      Recharge

      Puts charge back in. Meant to feel slow.

      The PU mode switch on the wheel picks between them: Q, RACE, and SLO or SC for recharge. Attack, Defend, Push and Save sit on AD1, AD2, K2 and ES. In the other modes STRAT Map on the pit setup screen decides.
